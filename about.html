The short version

Hi — I design and build the kind of interfaces I'd want to use myself.

I started as a front-end developer, got frustrated translating other people's flat designs into motion, and eventually took over the design side entirely. Now I do both — which means fewer meetings and a lot less lost in translation.

Outside of client work, I write about interface animation and occasionally teach a workshop on scroll-based storytelling.
